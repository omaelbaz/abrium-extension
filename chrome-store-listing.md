## 3. Detailed Description
Abrium is a free, 100% local, open-source **claude artifacts manager** with zero tracking. It automatically captures the valuable code, SVGs, and documents generated in your conversations, allowing you to organize your work entirely on your own device. Your data never leaves your browser.

Are you looking to **save claude ai code** without tedious copying and pasting? This **claude.ai export extension** runs silently in the background while you chat, building a visual gallery of your creations. When you're ready, trigger a **claude artifact download** to export everything in a single, organized ZIP file.

**Key Features:**
- **Automatic Capture**: Silently extracts and saves artifacts as you browse your claude.ai chats.
- **Visual Gallery**: Browse, search, and manage all your generated content in a beautiful Terracotta-themed side panel.
- **Batch Export**: Download individual files or export your entire artifact library with a single click.
- **Pin & Organize**: Pin your most important artifacts to keep them at the top of your list.
- **100% Private**: Your data is stored only in your browser's local storage. No accounts, no servers, and absolutely no analytics or tracking.

**Supported Languages:**
Abrium includes full native UI support for:
- English (en)
- Arabic (ar)
- French (fr)
- Spanish (es)
- Portuguese (pt)

**Open Source & Trust:**
Abrium is proudly open-source and licensed under the MIT License. Our commitment to privacy is absolute—what you generate on Claude stays on your machine. Check out our GitHub repository to view the source code or contribute.
